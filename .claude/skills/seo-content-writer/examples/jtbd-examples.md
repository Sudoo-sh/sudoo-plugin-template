# JTBD Examples by Industry

Use these as templates when defining the Job-to-Be-Done for your article.

**Format:** *"When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [desired outcome]."*

---

## B2B SaaS

- When we're evaluating whether to build or buy a new feature, I want to understand how other SaaS teams have made this decision, so I can bring a structured framework to the discussion with our CTO.

- When our churn starts creeping up, I want to understand what leading indicators other companies track, so I can catch the problem before it shows up in revenue.

- When I'm preparing our annual pricing review, I want to understand how SaaS companies successfully raised prices without losing customers, so I can build confidence in our proposal to leadership.

- When a potential enterprise client asks about our security posture, I want to understand what SOC 2 compliance actually involves, so I can give an accurate answer and assess whether we need it.

---

## Marketing & Agencies

- When a client asks why their Google Ads aren't converting, I want to quickly diagnose the most common reasons, so I can walk into the next call with a credible hypothesis and action plan.

- When we're planning the next lifecycle marketing phase, I want to evaluate whether gamification would improve retention, so I can present a clear case to the growth team.

- When I realize our content isn't ranking despite good writing, I want to understand what technical SEO issues might be holding us back, so I can prioritize fixes without hiring a specialist.

- When I'm building a B2B content strategy from scratch, I want to understand what content formats actually generate pipeline, so I can avoid investing in channels that don't convert.

---

## IT Outsourcing & Dev Services

- When we're considering switching from a fixed-price to T&M model, I want to understand how other agencies have made the transition, so I can anticipate client objections and set up the right contracts.

- When a prospect asks how we handle communication across time zones, I want to understand best practices from distributed teams, so I can turn a potential objection into a differentiator.

- When a client project is heading toward scope creep, I want to understand how other PMs have handled it diplomatically, so I can protect our margins without damaging the relationship.

---

## HR & People Ops

- When we're redesigning our performance review process, I want to understand what alternatives to annual reviews actually work, so I can propose something the leadership team will approve and employees won't dread.

- When an employee resigns unexpectedly, I want to understand what early warning signals we might have missed, so I can build a retention system that catches disengagement earlier.

---

## Finance & FinTech

- When our startup is approaching Series A, I want to understand what financial metrics investors actually scrutinize, so I can start tracking the right things now and avoid surprises in due diligence.

- When a customer asks about fraud protection, I want to give a clear, honest explanation of what our system catches and what it doesn't, so I can build trust without overpromising.

---

## Usage Notes

- One JTBD per article. Don't try to serve multiple reader situations in a single piece.
- The "so I can" part is the most important — it defines the **real outcome** the reader wants, not just the topic they searched for.
- If you can't write a clear JTBD statement, the article topic is probably too broad. Narrow it.
